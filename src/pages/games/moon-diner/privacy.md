# Privacy Policy

**Moon Diner: Space Tycoon**

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Moon Diner: Space Tycoon ("Moon Diner") is a single-player tycoon game made by Ruslan Akhmetvaleev ("we"). It has no accounts and no servers, and it does not collect, store or share any personal information.

**Your game progress.** Coins, opened rooms, upgrades, Galaxy Stars, the planet you are on and your settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

**No ads.** The game shows no ads and contains no advertising code, so it does not use your device's advertising identifier.

**No statistics or crash reports.** The game has no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. It does not send anything about how you play, your device or your location to us or to anyone else.

**No internet.** The game does not connect to the internet and plays fully offline.

**Purchases.** The game has no in-app purchases.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

**Changes.** If a later version adds ads, purchases, analytics or anything else that handles data, we will update this page and the store privacy details before that version is released.

**Contact.** Questions: support@licman.dev
