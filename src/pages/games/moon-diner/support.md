# Support

**Moon Diner: Space Tycoon**

Found a bug, stuck on a planet, or have an idea? Email **[support@licman.dev](mailto:support@licman.dev)** with your phone model and the planet you are on (it's on the chip at the top of the screen). We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I move?** Drag anywhere on the screen. Stand still on a glowing spot to pick up or drop food, and stop at a machine to upgrade it.

**The Warp Drive says LOCKED.** Each planet needs certain rooms open before you can warp. Stand on the Warp Drive pad in the Chef Room to see which rooms are still missing; the gold arrow points to the next one.

**Why did my stack fall over?** It's low gravity: a tall stack wobbles when you turn fast. Gravity Boots and the Anti-Grav Grip upgrade, both in the Chef Room, calm the wobble.

**Where is my progress saved?** On your phone only. There is no account, so progress can't move between phones, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

**How do I delete my data?** Uninstall the game. We don't keep any data on our side; the game has no ads, analytics or internet connection. See the [privacy policy](/games/moon-diner/privacy/).

**Are there ads or purchases?** No. Moon Diner is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
