# Privacy Policy

**Shelf Brawlers**

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Shelf Brawlers is a single-player game made by Ruslan Akhmetvaleev ("we"). We have no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

**Your game progress.** Coins, unlocked figures, stickers and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Ads.** The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example a revive or double Coins) and occasional ads between runs. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), your approximate location from your IP address, and information about ads you see and tap, plus crash and performance data. How Google uses this is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Game statistics.** The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous game events — for example a run started, the floor reached, a boss beaten or an ad watched — together with an app-instance identifier, your device model, OS version, country and approximate region (from your IP address). We use this only to see how players progress and to improve the game; it is not linked to your name or any account, and it is processed by Google ([firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)).

**Crash reports.** If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: what went wrong in the game's code, the game version, your device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs; it is not linked to your name or any account, and it is processed by Google ([firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)).

**Feedback emails.** If you use "Send feedback" in Settings, your email app opens with the game version and your phone's system version filled in. We only receive what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you and improve the game.

**Your choices.** You can refuse tracking in the prompt, or later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; you will then see non-personalised ads. In the EU and UK the game asks for your consent with Google's form, and you can change it any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

**Purchases.** If the game sells in-app items, Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

**Changes.** We will update this page and the App Store and Google Play privacy details before a version that changes how data is handled is released.

**Contact.** Questions: [support@licman.dev](mailto:support@licman.dev)
