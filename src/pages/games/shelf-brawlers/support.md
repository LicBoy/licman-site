# Support

**Shelf Brawlers**

Found a bug, stuck somewhere, or have an idea? Email **[support@licman.dev](mailto:support@licman.dev)** with your phone model and what happened. The quickest way is **Settings → Send feedback** in the game: it fills in the game version and your phone's system for you.

## Common questions

**No sound?** Check the volume. On iPhone, turn on "Sound in silent mode" in Settings (the gear on the Shelf).

**Where is my progress saved?** On your phone only. There is no account, so progress can't move between phones, and deleting the game deletes it.

**How do I delete my data?** Delete the game. We don't keep any game data on our side; see the [privacy policy](/games/shelf-brawlers/privacy/) for what Google's ads and analytics services collect and how to reset your advertising ID.

**How do I change my ad consent?** In the EU and UK, open Settings → Privacy options in the game.
