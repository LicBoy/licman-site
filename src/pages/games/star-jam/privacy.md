# Privacy Policy

**Star Jam: Rocket Escape**

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Star Jam: Rocket Escape ("Star Jam") is a single-player puzzle game made by Ruslan Akhmetvaleev ("we"). We have no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

**Your game progress.** Coins, beaten levels and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Ads.** The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example an extra dock slot, a free booster or double coins) and occasional ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, information about ads you see and tap, and crash and performance data. How Google uses this is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

**Game statistics.** The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous game events — for example a level started, won or lost, a booster used or an ad watched — together with an app-instance identifier, your device model, OS version, country and approximate region (from your IP address). We use this only to see how players progress and to improve the game; it is not linked to your name or any account, and it is processed by Google (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

**Crash reports.** If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: what went wrong in the game's code, the game version, your device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs; it is not linked to your name or any account, and it is processed by Google.

**Your choices.** In the EU and UK the game asks for your consent with Google's form, and you can change it any time with "Privacy options" on the game's home screen. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone, you can refuse tracking in the prompt, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; you will then see non-personalised ads.

**Purchases.** If the game sells in-app items, Google or Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

**Changes.** We will update this page and the store privacy details before a version that changes how data is handled is released.

**Contact.** Questions: support@licman.dev
