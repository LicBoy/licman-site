# Support

**Star Jam: Rocket Escape**

Found a bug, stuck on a level, or have an idea? Email **[support@licman.dev](mailto:support@licman.dev)** with your phone model and the level number. We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**Is every level winnable?** Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before it ships. If you are stuck, try a different first launch: the order matters more than speed. Power-ups (U-turn, tractor beam, bomb) and boosters help on the hardest levels.

**Where is my progress saved?** On your phone only. There is no account, so progress can't move between phones, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

**How do I delete my data?** Uninstall the game. We don't keep any game data on our side; see the [privacy policy](/games/star-jam/privacy/) for what Google's ads and analytics services collect and how to reset your advertising ID.

**How do I change my ad consent?** In the EU and UK, tap "Privacy options" on the game's home screen.

**The sound is too loud or annoying.** Tap the speaker button on the home screen to mute.
